# The shopping story for the demo (Visa track). Owned by User 1.
#
#   PowerShell:  $env:SHOP_SHIP_DELAY_MS=2000; $env:SHOP_DELIVER_DELAY_MS=4000; npm run sim -- data/demo-shopping.txt
#   Git Bash:    SHOP_SHIP_DELAY_MS=2000 SHOP_DELIVER_DELAY_MS=4000 npm run sim -- data/demo-shopping.txt
#
# Uses the example family (Nai Nai zh, Kevin en, Mei en + trusted for money) and the curated
# catalog. IDs like #b10 assume no AI key (placeholder translations, instant). With a real key,
# Nai Nai's cards arrive a few seconds later and the IDs shift.
#
# Story: need → 3 options as cards in everyone's language → the family talks it over (threaded
# on the cards) → votes by tapback and letter → checkout (buyer's ❤️ on the card is her yes) →
# confirmation, shipped, delivered → "send a photo of the manual".

# ── 1. Discovery + personalization ──────────────────────────────────────
# Nai Nai: "My blood pressure monitor broke, I want a new one with big numbers."
# Relayed as chat; Bridge sends everyone a heading + cards A/B/C (Kevin b3 b6 b9 b12,
# Mei b4 b7 b10 b13, Nai Nai b5 b8 b11 b14).
nainai: 我的血压计坏了，想买个新的，字要大一点
/wait 1500

# ── 2. Decision-making: the family talks it over ────────────────────────
# A bare "ok" isn't a vote: it's relayed as chat.
kevin: ok
/wait 300
# A threaded question on Mei's card B threads onto card B in Kevin's and Nai Nai's threads.
mei: reply b10 is B too heavy for her?
/wait 300
kevin: reply b9 no, it's light, and the numbers are huge
/wait 300

# ── 3. Votes ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Mei ❤️ her card B: a vote for B, and (she's the buyer) her yes to B.
mei: tapback b10 love
/wait 300
# Nai Nai replies "B": two of three is a majority, so B wins. Votes are never relayed.
nainai: B
/wait 1500

# ── 4–6. Checkout, payment, post-purchase ───────────────────────────────
# Under Mei's limit and she ❤️ the winning card, so Bridge checks out with no second question.
# Everyone gets "✅ … ordered … for Nai Nai, arriving …" in their language; then Mei and Nai Nai
# get "📦 Shipped" and "🏠 Delivered / It arrived! Send me a photo of the manual…".
/wait 5000
